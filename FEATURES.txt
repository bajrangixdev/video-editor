QUALITY LEVELS  (run.bat: P / H / F / U   or   --quality name)
======================================================================
  preview  1280x720  30fps  crf 20  preset veryfast
  hd       1920x1080  30fps  crf 17  preset fast
  final    1920x1080  60fps  crf 15  preset slow
  uhd      2560x1440  60fps  crf 15  preset medium
  (lower crf = better quality + bigger file.  'final' 1080p60 is the one to upload to YouTube)

======================================================================
THE 11 TECHNIQUES (Decoding YT 'Techniques Used')
======================================================================
  1 Line Animation     glowing lines / arrows / frames that draw themselves   {line: x1,y1,x2,y2}  {frame: x,y,w,h}  (also auto under neon text and around the main card)
  2 3D Moving Layer    cards keep swinging in 3D perspective (channel cards, thumbnails, list pills) + tilted dashboards with a sunburst
  3 Shake Effect       camera shake + item shake on impact   {shake: 1}  (auto on number cards, titles, shocked pose)
  4 Shine Effect       a glossy light band sweeps across cards, logos, titles every ~2.5 s
  5 Glowing Text       neon / heavy / gradient text with a pulsing glow
  6 Grid Motion BG     two line grids scrolling at different speeds (parallax)   {fx: mgrid|spot}
  7 3D Rotating Logo   YouTube logo spinning in 3D with shading   {prop: youtube}
  8 Number Animation   numbers count up (small cards and the giant counter) with coin / confetti burst at the end
  9 Move Along Path    any prop or thumbnail flies along a curved path with a glow trail   {fly: youtube|magnifier|bell|thumb}
  10 Null Layer        invisible controller that moves / zooms / rotates a whole group of items together (thumbnail group, list)
  11 Head Movement     the suit character's head tilts, bobs and nods on spoken words, separate from the body

======================================================================
CAMERA  (v6)
======================================================================
  automatic SHOT VARIATION: every scene gets a framing (wide/medium/close) + move (push, pull, pan_l, pan_r, tilt_u, tilt_d, drift, punch, orbit, static); never the same twice in a row
  long scenes get a SNAP-ZOOM punch-in on the strongest spoken word;  smooth HANDHELD drift;  TRAUMA SHAKE (decays smoothly) + RGB split on impact
  CAMERA MOTION BLUR (sub-frame sampling)  +  2.5D PARALLAX: background / cards / character / foreground move at different speeds
  DEPTH OF FIELD: soft background with a focus pull at the start of every scene + foreground bokeh lights

======================================================================
PHOTO ANIMATION  (v6)  - put photos in the  photos  folder
======================================================================
  KEN BURNS      smooth zoom + pan over a still photo:  in out left right up down diag orbit          {kb: ...}
  2.5D PARALLAX  photo split into back / middle / front layers by a depth map (your own  name_depth.png  or automatic)   {parallax: 0..2}
  DEPTH OF FIELD back layer inpainted + blurred, focus pull between front and back                        {dof: fg|pull|pullfg|none}
  MASK REVEALS   a shape wipes the next photo in:  circle rrect wipe_l wipe_r wipe_u wipe_d diag diag2 bars_h bars_v blinds clock diamond split_h split_v          {reveal: ...}
  CARD MODE      photo in a rounded glowing frame                                                         {photostyle: card}
  AUTO           photos in the folder are used automatically in ~75% of the generic scenes (config.json -> photos)

TEXT ANIMATION  (v7)  - {text: Your words} {textfx: ...}
======================================================================
  1 kinetic     words fly / slam / spin in, a different move for each word (*word* = accent colour)      {textfx: kinetic}
  2 typewriter  terminal panel, letters typed with key clicks, blinking caret                            {textfx: typewriter}
  3 glow        neon tube that flickers on, then pulses (cyan -> pink)                                   {textfx: glow}
  4 extrude     3D extruded letters, depth grows, light angle swings                                     {textfx: extrude}
  5 reveal      colour bar sweeps each line, then uncovers the text                                      {textfx: reveal}
  6 tracking    letter-spacing animates wide -> tight with blur-in                                       {textfx: tracking}
  7 bounce      letters drop with bounce + squash, then a jumping wave                                   {textfx: bounce}
  8 shake       slam + shake bursts with red/cyan split (bursts land on emphasised words)                {textfx: shake}
  9 morph       one text melts into the next (liquid blob morph).  {text: A -> B -> C}                    {textfx: morph}
 10 mask        text is a window onto moving colour or a photo                                           {textfx: mask} {textfill: photo.jpg|aurora}
 11 rotate3d    solid extruded text spinning in real 3D perspective                                      {textfx: rotate3d}
 12 path        letters ride a path: wave arc circle spiral sline                                {textfx: path} {textpath: wave}
 13 liquid      water-like distortion + colour fringe that settles                                       {textfx: liquid}
 14 shine       gold text with a light sweep + sparkles                                                  {textfx: shine}
 15 subtitles   animated captions: karaoke | pop | bar | glow | classic     {captions: pop}   (default in config.json -> caption_style)
  extras: {textcolor: 255,200,40}  {textsize: 1.3}   {textfx: auto} picks a different effect each scene.  Every effect plays its own sound effects + camera hits.

======================================================================
AUDIO  (v6)
======================================================================
  AUDIO-SYNCED ANIMATION: items appear when the narrator SAYS them; captions pop harder on emphasised words;
    camera punches on emphasis; character / icons bob with the voice loudness
  SOUND: stereo, 26 sounds (59 files incl. variants - the mixer picks one at random), tiny random pitch/volume, panned to the picture, ducked under the voice,
    reverb on big hits, attack aligned to the visual hit, voice high-pass + compressor, optional MUSIC BED (assets/music, auto-ducks), mastered to -14 LUFS

======================================================================
SMOOTHNESS  (v6)
======================================================================
  sub-pixel placement for everything (no 1-pixel stutter), spring physics on pops, per-item + camera motion blur, scrolling layers at float precision, light film grain (kills banding)
  turn effects off in config.json -> effects, or add --fast for the quickest test render
